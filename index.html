AI-Augmented Practice

An early adopter since
December 2022.

Among the first wave of ChatGPT power users in India. I use AI to compress research, synthesis, and ideation cycles while preserving rigour — directly impacting velocity on multi-month transformation engagements. The distinction that matters: prompt-level usage (structured multi-shot prompts with explicit format constraints) versus workflow-level design (persistent system prompts and cumulative project context that make AI sustainable across long, complex programmes without drift).

Research & Synthesis

Interview synthesis at speed and coverage

Structured prompts to analyse transcripts, cluster themes, and generate insight hierarchies from raw user data. What used to take a full day of affinity mapping now takes an hour.

Prompt Engineering

Multi-shot prompts with format constraints

I write prompts the way I write design specs — with explicit output format, examples, edge cases, and constraints. Consistent, repeatable quality across 110+ journey projects.

Workflow Design

System prompts that carry project memory

Persistent system prompts that front-load design system vocabulary, component rules, and project constraints. Every AI call is informed by cumulative context — not a blank slate.

About

The designer
behind the work

I'm a design-trained business problem solver — pattern recognizer, systems thinker, operator inside complexity. My background spans a B.Tech in Information Technology and an M.Des from IIT Delhi, which means I move comfortably between engineering constraints, regulatory compliance, and business strategy. I'm also AI-augmented by default — early ChatGPT adopter since December 2022, using structured prompts and persistent context architecture to compress research and synthesis cycles without losing rigour.

My natural fit is design-led digital transformation — the kind of work where the design problem is inseparable from the business and operational problem. My career has been spent inside that overlap: multi-channel journeys spanning apps, web portals, and physical bank branches simultaneously; insurance products requiring clearance from 11 internal SBI stakeholder teams; pattern-libraries that govern 40+ products through systems leverage rather than per-product execution. I also contributed to a VR research project for the Indian Armed Forces under IIT Delhi — a proof-of-concept later presented to the President of India.

Outside of product work, I mentor CEED aspirants — 35+ mentees have been selected into IITs and NIDs.
